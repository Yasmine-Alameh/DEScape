How to Play DEScape

Important: DES is studied here because it is historically important and teaches how block ciphers are built. It is not secure for modern use: only 56 of its 64 key bits are effective, which makes exhaustive key search (brute-force attacks) practical.

    Your progress is saved to your account.

    Start an adventure

    Guided: step-by-step lessons and free hints. Challenge: shorter instructions, recorded as an independent result.

  3. 3
    Intro & lobby

    The lab locks down and NOVA explains the mission.
